WooCommerce tells you what your store did. Customer History tells you who your customers are — and what they're doing.

== Description ==

<p><strong>Customer History 3.0.0 is a complete revamp</strong> — rebuilt from the ground up as a focused customer-intelligence and reporting tool for WooCommerce. We've moved away from scattered actions and doubled down on one thing: showing you exactly what your customers are doing on your store — who's buying, who's browsing without buying, and what they're searching for — so you can plan your next move with real insight instead of guesswork. The analytics are read-only and run entirely on your own server, and the plugin never contacts your customers automatically — it sends no bulk or marketing emails.</p>

<p>Most WooCommerce stores fly blind on their customers. The native dashboard reports orders, revenue, and traffic — but not <em>who</em> is buying, who's browsing without buying, what people are searching for, or which searches return nothing. PureDevs Customer History turns raw store activity into <strong>customer intelligence</strong>: profiles, search insight, view tracking, and segments — all inside wp-admin.</p>

<p>Tracking starts the moment you activate it: no wizard, no configuration, works out of the box. And it's private by design — all data stays in your own WordPress database, with nothing sent to any third-party service or API.</p>

<h4>WHAT CUSTOMER HISTORY SHOWS YOU</h4>

<p><strong>Customer Intelligence Dashboard.</strong> Your store's customer nerve centre. See total customers, new customers this period, and active customers at a glance, plus widgets for top customers by lifetime value, latest orders with customer context, top-selling products, top search terms, top categories, recent searches, and most-viewed products — with a date-range picker and period-over-period comparison.</p>

<p><strong>Customer List &amp; Profiles.</strong> A customer-first view of your store. Sort and search your customers by name, email, orders, lifetime spend, and last order date. Open any customer to see their full profile: financial summary, complete order history, that customer's recent searches, and recently viewed products — data WooCommerce native simply does not provide.</p>

<p><strong>Customer Notes.</strong> A private notepad on every customer profile. Jot down what the numbers cannot tell you — a phone call, a complaint, a delivery preference — and it stays with that customer. Notes are admin-only, never shown to the customer, and are included in the WordPress personal-data export and erase tools. Annotation only: no tasks, no reminders, no follow-ups.</p>

<p><strong>Search &amp; Discovery Analytics.</strong> A dedicated Search Analytics page with nothing comparable in WooCommerce. See your top search terms and most recent searches — so you know exactly what shoppers are looking for — alongside the most-viewed products with view counts and your top viewed categories.</p>

<p><strong>Zero-Result Searches.</strong> The searches that came back empty — demand your catalogue failed to answer. The Search Analytics page lists every term that returned no products, how often it was searched, and when it was last searched, so you can see what shoppers wanted and you did not have. WooCommerce cannot see this, and neither can any report built from orders alone.</p>

<p><strong>View Tracking.</strong> Passive, lightweight tracking of which products and categories your customers view. The view-to-purchase gap is invisible to WooCommerce native; Customer History surfaces it on every customer profile and on the Search Analytics page.</p>

<p><strong>Revenue.</strong> A focused revenue dashboard: net revenue, orders, average order value, customers, and your repeat purchase rate — the share of customers in the period who bought more than once — for the selected period (within the free 90-day window), each with period-over-period comparison, plus a revenue trend chart.</p>

<p><strong>Order History.</strong> A store-wide order list you can filter by date range, order status, and keyword, with quick links straight into the matching WooCommerce order edit screens — a fast way to find and cross-reference orders alongside everything else you know about the customer.</p>

<p><strong>Manual Customer Email.</strong> When you want to follow up with a single customer, you can compose and send them a one-off email straight from their profile. It's entirely manual and admin-initiated — there are no automated sends, no bulk emails, and no marketing campaigns. You write each message yourself, and the plugin never contacts a customer unless you choose to.</p>

<p><strong>Customer Segments.</strong> Save a customer segment configuration for quick recall. The Segments page shows your saved segment with a live customer count and one-click CSV export.</p>

<p><strong>Email Digest — Weekly or Monthly.</strong> An optional, opt-in summary email sent to you, the store owner: new customers, top search terms, top customers, and most-viewed products. Choose the interval in Settings — weekly, or monthly for a slower-moving store — and the report window follows your choice. Keeps you informed about customer activity even when you don't log in. (Admin-only — the digest goes to you, the store owner, not to your customers.)</p>

<p><strong>CSV Export.</strong> Export your customer list to CSV at any time.</p>

<h4>FREE VERSION SCOPE</h4>

All data — for every customer and every session — is collected from day one. The free version simply shows the most recent 100 customers and 90-day window; the full view is available in Pro.</p>

<h4>PRIVATE BY DESIGN</h4>

<p>Customer History is built around observation and reporting. It runs no automation, sends no bulk or marketing emails, creates no coupons, and never modifies orders, product data, or any WooCommerce setting. The one outbound action it offers is fully optional and manual: you can send an individual customer a one-off email that you compose yourself from their profile — nothing is ever sent automatically. It's HPOS-compatible, supports the WordPress GDPR export/erase workflow, and keeps all data on your own server.</p>

== Installation ==

Manual installation takes less than a minute.

1. Download the plugin from wordpress.org, unpack it, and upload the <strong>[PureDevs Customer History for WooCommerce]</strong> folder to your <strong>wp-content/plugins/</strong> directory — or install it directly from the Plugins screen in WordPress.
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu in WordPress.
3. Go to <strong>Customer History &gt; Settings</strong> to configure tracking options (optional — defaults work out of the box).

Data collection begins the moment the plugin activates. No setup wizard or configuration is required. You are good to go.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

<strong>Q. Is this plugin only for WordPress sites?</strong><br>
A. Yes. The plugin works with WordPress sites that have WooCommerce installed and activated. WooCommerce is required.

<strong>Q. Where is my customer data stored?</strong><br>
A. All data is stored in your own WordPress database. The plugin never sends customer data to any external service, API, or third-party server. Your data stays on your own server.

<strong>Q. Is there a video walkthrough?</strong><br>
A. Yes. You can watch a full walkthrough of the dashboard, customer profiles, and search analytics here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anFJa63GnfI

<strong>Q. Is there a live demo I can try?</strong><br>
A. Yes. Log in to https://ch.puredevs.com/wp-admin with username <code>demo</code> and password <code>demo1234</code>. The demo runs the Pro version, so you can explore every feature. The account is view-only — you can browse all screens but cannot add or edit anything.

<strong>Q. Is the plugin compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)?</strong><br>
A. Yes. The plugin uses WooCommerce CRUD methods and declares HPOS compatibility. It does not query the posts table directly for orders.

<strong>Q. Will this plugin slow down my store?</strong><br>
A. No. Session and view tracking writes are lightweight and happen passively during normal page loads. Heavy analytics queries run on demand and cache their results. You can configure the plugin to exclude bot and admin sessions to further reduce data volume.

<strong>Q. What customer data is stored, and how do I erase it for GDPR?</strong><br>
A. The plugin stores session data in your WordPress database: page views, search queries, and product views associated with each customer. No data leaves your server. To export or erase a customer's data, use the built-in WordPress tools at Tools &gt; Export Personal Data and Tools &gt; Erase Personal Data — the plugin fully supports the WordPress privacy request workflow.

<strong>Q. Does the plugin send emails to my customers?</strong><br>
A. The plugin never sends automated, bulk, or marketing emails to your customers, and it runs no campaigns. The digest — weekly or monthly, your choice — is an admin-only email delivered to you, the store owner. It does include one optional manual tool: from a customer's profile you can compose and send a single one-off email to that one customer yourself. Nothing is ever sent automatically — a customer is only ever emailed if you write and send the message.

<strong>Q. Does the plugin modify orders, products, or WooCommerce settings?</strong><br>
A. No. The plugin is observational where your store data is concerned. It never modifies orders, creates coupons, changes product data, or alters any WooCommerce setting.

<strong>Q. Is the plugin affected by the reported Freemius SDK vulnerabilities?</strong><br>
A. No. The plugin bundles Freemius WordPress SDK version 2.13.4. The two publicly reported Freemius SDK vulnerabilities were fixed in SDK versions 1.0.1 and 1.0.2, long before the version shipped here, so this plugin is not affected.

<strong>Q. What does the free version include?</strong><br>
A. The free version shows up to 100 customers in the customer list and the most recent 90 days of analytics, with the full Customer Intelligence Dashboard, customer profiles, private customer notes, Search Analytics including zero-result searches, view tracking, a Revenue dashboard with a repeat-purchase-rate card, an Order History page with date/status/keyword filters, one saved segment, the weekly or monthly digest, a manual one-to-one customer email tool, and CSV export. All data is captured from day one; upgrading to Pro unlocks the full customer list, full history, and the advanced intelligence features.

<strong>Q. Can customers see the notes I write on their profile?</strong><br>
A. No. Customer notes are private to your store's admin area and are only visible to users who can manage WooCommerce. They are never displayed on the front end and never emailed to anyone. They are stored in your own database and are included in the WordPress personal-data export and erase tools, so a GDPR request covers them.

<strong>Q. Why is the Zero-Result Searches list empty on a busy store?</strong><br>
A. Because it starts from the day you upgrade. Recording whether a search returned results is new, so the plugin cannot say what older searches returned. The panel tells you the date collection began, and terms appear as visitors search from that point onward.

<strong>Q. Do I need any technical knowledge to use the plugin?</strong><br>
A. No. Just install and activate — no coding required. All data is collected automatically from your WooCommerce store.
