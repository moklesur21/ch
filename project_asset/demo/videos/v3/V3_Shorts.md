## SHORT 1 — Same order value, completely different customer
*≈45 s · the batch's plugin-mention slot*

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
- **On-screen text (5 words):** SAME ORDER. COMPLETELY DIFFERENT CUSTOMER.
- **Spoken:** "Two customers placed the same order on your store this morning. In your order list — identical."

**PROOF (one demo clip)**
- **[SCREEN CLIP: Customers & Users — the two matching-value rows, then both profiles side by side. Viewer should notice the *gap* between the two RFM bar sets and financial summaries, not any total.]**
- **Spoken (no figures):** "But open the same two people in a customer-intelligence view… and look at the gap. One relationship is long, steady, and climbing. The other just began. Read the proportion, not the number — same order, two completely different futures for your store."

**LANDING (one line — plugin named, once)**
- **Spoken:** "PureDevs Customer History puts value, health, and lifecycle on the same row — so identical orders stop hiding different customers."

**Caption text:** Same order value. Completely different customer. Your order list can't tell them apart — your customer list can. (Pro view shown · free plugin on WordPress.org — link in description.)

## SHORT 2 — What a WooCommerce Champion actually means
*≈50 s · pure insight, no plugin name*

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
- **On-screen text (5 words):** WHAT A CHAMPION ACTUALLY MEANS
- **Spoken:** "Somewhere in your WooCommerce customer list there's a small group quietly carrying the store. The industry name for them: Champions."

**PROOF (one demo clip)**
- **[SCREEN CLIP: the customer list filtering down to Champions — badges visible. Viewer should notice the list collapsing to a short column and how much of the spend column sits inside it.]**
- **Spoken (no figures):** "A Champion isn't your biggest single order. It's three signals landing together: bought recently, buys often, spends well when they do. It's one of ten named segments customer-intelligence tools score straight from your order history. Watch the list collapse when you filter for all three — and read the proportion of spend sitting inside that short column. That's the store's real backbone."

**LANDING (one line — question)**
- **Spoken:** "So — does anyone in that group get treated differently than a first-time coupon-chaser?"

**Caption text:** "Champion" = recent + frequent + high-value, together. A label only matters if it changes how you treat someone. Does yours?

## SHORT 3 — RFM without the jargon
*≈45 s · pure insight, no plugin name*

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
- **On-screen text (4 words):** RFM, MINUS THE JARGON
- **Spoken:** "RFM sounds like consultant-speak. It's actually three questions your store can already answer."

**PROOF (one demo clip)**
- **[SCREEN CLIP: one customer profile's RFM bars, held steady. Viewer should notice the three bars and their relative lengths — never the numbers.]**
- **Spoken (no figures):** "Recency — when did they last buy? Frequency — how often do they come back? Monetary — how much do they spend when they do? Score every customer on all three, and the anonymous list turns into a ranked one: who's rising, who's fading, who quietly matters most. On screen, don't read the numbers — read the bar lengths."

**LANDING (one line — insight)**
- **Spoken:** "You don't need new data for any of this. Your orders already answered all three questions — most stores just never ask."

**Caption text:** Recency. Frequency. Monetary. Three questions your order history already answers — most stores never ask them.
