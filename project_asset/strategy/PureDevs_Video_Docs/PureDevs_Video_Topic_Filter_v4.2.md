## 1. What reading the strategy changed

### 1.1 Format
v4.0's hook/reveal/proof structure, its 20-second Free-vs-Pro block, and its **6 s and 15 s cuts are withdrawn**. The strategy sets one 20–30 s cut and warns that videos under 11 seconds do not build a remarketing list. v4.0's series line ("Your reports start at the order…") is also dropped: it does not fit V1, V3 or V7, and the strategy already has a closing voice ("The plugin doesn't decide for you. It shows you where the money comes from.").

### 1.2 Two lines from v4.0 that break the strategy's claims discipline
"No sentence in a video without the screen that proves it" (§4.5) removes:
- the WooCommerce 11 cart-reminder remark in the funnel video. No screen proves it, and it sits next to recovery language;
- the "when SaaS is the better choice" narration in the data video. The factual model comparison belongs in the LP-Alternatives **page text** (checklist D2), not in the voice-over.

### 1.3 V1 has real evidence behind it
v4.0 ranked "new vs returning" low as commodity order maths. That was too harsh. A WordPress.org review of WooCommerce Analytics (28 Oct 2025) asks for exactly this: comparative data for new vs returning customers over a date range. Core lets you **filter** the Orders report by new or returning; it does not put the two side by side. V1 stays the hero for AG1, where searchers type "customer analytics" and expect a customer answer.

### 1.4 What stands from v4.0's research
- The official Marketplace sells order-based customer analytics at **$79/yr** (Coddium, v2.7.2): RFM, CLV, cohorts, churn, repeat rate, plus coupon and email actions. It adds no storefront tracking. The never-pitch list is right, and on-site behaviour (searches, views, funnel) is where we are alone.
- Buyers expect cart **recovery**; ours observes. The strategy already holds back "abandoned cart" keywords for this reason. v4.1 moves V5 out of this window (§5).
- "Viral" here means shared in groups because the viewer can **check their own store free**. That applies to V13, V14 and V15. V1, V8 and V16 are closers and will not be shared. The strategy's scorecard does not count views, and neither does this file.

## 3. Format — the strategy's, plus three small additions

**From the strategy (unchanged):** 60–120 s · one question · founder voice · burned-in captions · 1080p screen recording, cursor highlighted, zoom on the number · seeded demo store only · "PureDevs Customer History" in the first frame and the end card · no intro animation over 2 s · one CTA.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The question | 0–5 s | The owner's question, in their words |
| 2. Where to click | 5–20 s | The path. Say once that the data never leaves the site |
| 3. What it shows | 20–60 s | Show the numbers, zoomed. Describe the proportion, never the figure (owner rule below) |
| 4. The decision | 60–90 s | "So here is the decision this gives you…" End on the decision, never the dashboard |
| 5. Next step | last 10 s | One CTA |

**Additions:**

1. **Beat 1 picture = the answer frame.** Speak the question over the finished screen, then walk the path in beat 2. The same frame, with the question as text, is the poster and thumbnail. This feeds gate G2 directly (video play rate on pricing ≥ 10 %).
2. **Beat 5 carries the Free/Pro boundary in one sentence**, in the Brief's wording ("Free shows…", never "Free only tracks…"). CTA: a free-answered question → install free. A Pro-answered question → start the trial.
3. **The 20–30 s cut** = beat 1 + the answer frame + the beat-4 sentence. It skips beat 2.

**Owner rules (they beat every other file):**

- **No spoken figures anywhere in the demo.** The narration says "read the proportion, not the number". It points at which card, bar or row is bigger and never recites an amount, count, percentage or date. This works on any demo store, survives values changing on recording day, and is stronger than reciting totals. Where a relationship could flip on the day, the script carries both options in `[brackets]`.
- **No 100-customer cap on Free.** Never mention one in any script, page, email or ad, even where Brief 3.1.2 lists it.
- **Freshness.** Any sentence about what WooCommerce core or another plugin does or does not do is checked against its latest version on the day it is written, or it is cut.

**Standing rules:** demo numbers are synthetic and never presented as a customer result. Never say "WooCommerce has no analytics". The plugin observes and reports; the owner acts.
