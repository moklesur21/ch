## H3. Reviews — after the listings are live

**Target (fills the strategy's `[REVIEW-TARGET]`): 5 Capterra reviews.** Five is where a rating starts to carry weight, and it is the number gate G3c points to. Three by 15 Nov is the realistic outcome, because the listing may not be live before mid-October.

**Who to ask, in this order**
1. Active Pro users with 30 or more days of use.
2. Free users who have replied to one of your emails. Free-version users are real users and may review.
3. Trial users who bought, 30 days after purchase.
4. Capterra's own review-collection service, once you are eligible. They send the request and carry any incentive, which keeps you clear of every rule below.

**Rules (unchanged from August, plus one)**
- You never offer anything for a review. Not a coupon, not an extension, not a licence.
- A review request never shares an email with an offer, and goes out weeks apart from one (Founding 50, TRY60, COMEBACK6).
- Ask for an honest review, in those words. One request, one nudge after 7 days, then stop. Log name, date asked, nudge, outcome.
- Send 1:1 from yeasir@puredevs.com. **New:** add the person to the Brevo list *Suppress — Personal* that week, so no automation lands on top of your note.
- Reply to every review within two days, including critical ones.

**The request**
> **Subject:** a small favour, only if you have five minutes
>
> Hi {{name}},
>
> You've been using Customer History for a while now, so your honest view of it is worth more to me than most.
>
> I've just listed it on Capterra, and a listing with no reviews is hard for other store owners to trust. If you have five minutes, would you leave a review here? [CAPTERRA REVIEW LINK]
>
> Honest feedback is fine, good or bad. There's nothing in it for you except my thanks, and I read every one.
>
> Yeasir

**Where reviews go once they exist** (strategy §8): one quote on the pricing page and on each question page, one line in the video end card, always with the reviewer's written permission. The hidden `pd/reviews` block on the landing pages switches on at three. **Do not put Capterra or G2 ratings into the site's schema markup**: Google does not allow ratings gathered from other sites to be marked up as your own.
