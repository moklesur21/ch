entry with the PDF file and page it starts on. It adds no content of its own; the brief is the source.

**Reading the page columns.** "Part 1 · p.13" means page 13 of the part-1 PDF. "Full" is the page in the
single-file PDF. Page numbers match the "n / total" printed in each page footer, and all three PDFs carry
the same headings as bookmarks. An entry's screenshots sit directly under its heading, captioned with
their filename (for example `DASH-01.png`). A screenshot referenced more than once is printed in full only
at its first mention in each file; later mentions read "(shown earlier)".

**§D (do-not-claim list) is mandatory reading before any copy or script is written.** It is in part 2.
