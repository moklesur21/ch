**Stop and report.** Do not record, or discard the take, if any of these happens:
- The wp.org card reads "Untested with your version of WordPress" on WordPress 7.1.3.
- Any prompt or notice appears other than the Freemius opt-in (S2) and the Dashboard upsell banner (S3).
- Skip does not land on Customer History → Dashboard.
- Search Analytics lacks "hoodie" or either product.
- Site Health's "First free-tier limit reached" is not "None reached yet".
